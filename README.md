# Sip

A tiny glass of water that lives in your Mac's menu bar.

I often forget to drink water when I'm deep in work, so I made a quiet reminder for myself. No pop-ups, no notifications. Just a subtle nudge, like checking your battery.

## How it works
- The glass starts full every time you open your Mac
- It slowly drains while you work
- Take a sip, click the glass, and it refills
- Ignore it too long and the glass cracks
- It pauses when your Mac isn't in use

## Install
1. Open `Sip.dmg` and drag **Sip** into your **Applications** folder
2. Right-click Sip → **Open** (the first time only)
3. If macOS still blocks it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**

Sip isn't from the App Store, so macOS asks for this once.
